Last updated: July 15, 2026

Introduction

Salem Innovative Ventures ("SIV", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Smart Music Player.

We use collected data to provide personalized playlists, improve features, and ensure app stability. We do not sell your personal data.

Your Rights

You can request data deletion or access by contacting support. We comply with applicable privacy laws including GDPR and CCPA.
