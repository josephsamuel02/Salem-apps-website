Delete Your Dream Diary Account

You can permanently delete your Dream Diary account directly from within the app. Please note that account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Dream Diary app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to the Account page.
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Read the confirmation message and tap Delete Account again to permanently delete your account.

What Will Be Deleted

The following data will be permanently removed:

  • Account information
  • Profile information
  • Journal entries
  • Mood history
  • Images uploaded to journal entries
  • Audio recordings attached to journal entries
  • App preferences and settings
  • Any other personal data associated with the account

Data Retention

Note: Some information may be retained where required by applicable law or for legitimate legal, security, or fraud prevention purposes.

Need Help?

If you encounter any issues while trying to delete your account or need further assistance, please contact our support team at support@sivdigital.com.
