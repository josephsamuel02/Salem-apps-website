Our Story

SIV Digital began with a simple frustration: why does powerful software have to feel heavy? Spreadsheets that crawl. Music apps that shout. Journals that treat your thoughts as data points.

We started Salem Innovative Ventures to prove the opposite that restraint is a feature. Small team, long timelines, obsessive attention to performance and privacy. Each app is crafted like an object: measured, durable, and quiet where it should be.

Mission

Craft essential software with the discipline of hardware.

Black and white. Fast and offline first. Private by default. Every decision removes complexity until only what matters remains.

Values

01 Restraint

We ship fewer features, but complete. No feature flags for the sake of it. If it doesn’t earn its place, it’s removed.

02 Performance

60fps, instant launch, offline true. Efficiency is not optional it’s respect for your time and battery.

03 Privacy

Local first, encrypted, no trackers. Your music, dreams and data never become our product.

04 Craft

Typography, spacing, haptics the last 10% is 90% of the feeling. We sweat the details you only feel.
