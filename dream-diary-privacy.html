Privacy Policy for Dream Diary

Last Updated: July 20, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Dream Diary.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Dream Diary collects, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have regarding your personal information.

By using Dream Diary, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Dream Diary may collect and store the following types of information:

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password (stored securely through our authentication provider)

Diary Content

When you use Dream Diary, you may choose to store:

  • Journal and diary entries
  • Notes and written content
  • Mood tracking information
  • Tags or categories associated with entries

Media Files

You may attach media to your diary entries, including:

  • Images
  • Audio recordings

These files are stored securely to provide the app's features.

Device Information

We may collect limited technical information such as:

  • App version
  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information (when available)

This information helps us improve the stability and performance of the app.

Fingerprint App Lock

Dream Diary offers an optional fingerprint (biometric) app lock feature.

Your fingerprint or other biometric information is never collected, stored, transmitted, or shared by Dream Diary.

Biometric authentication is handled entirely by your device's operating system using its secure biometric framework. Dream Diary only receives confirmation that authentication was successful or unsuccessful.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Synchronize your diary across your devices
  • Save your journal entries
  • Store images and audio recordings you choose to upload
  • Restore your data when you sign in
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain the security of our services

We do not use your diary content for advertising or profiling.

Data Storage

Your information is stored using secure cloud services and protected using industry standard security practices.

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to help protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication
  • Access controls
  • Industry standard security practices

Although we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We only share information when necessary to:

  • Operate and maintain the app
  • Provide cloud storage and authentication services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or others

Third Party Services

Dream Diary may use trusted third party service providers to operate the app, including services for:

  • User authentication
  • Secure cloud database storage
  • File storage
  • Analytics and diagnostics (if enabled)

These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Update your account information
  • Delete your diary entries
  • Delete your account
  • Request deletion of your associated personal data

You can permanently delete your account directly within the Dream Diary app by navigating to:

Account → Scroll to the bottom → Delete Account

Deleting your account permanently removes your personal data associated with Dream Diary, except where we are legally required to retain certain information.

You can also check our detailed account deletion guide for more information.

Children's Privacy

Dream Diary is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law without parental or guardian consent.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services or comply with legal obligations.

If you delete your account, your personal information and associated diary data will be permanently deleted within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate security or fraud prevention purposes.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of Dream Diary after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at:

Email: support@sivdigital.com
